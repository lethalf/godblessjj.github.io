Happy Birthday

Right on this day, you turn twenty.

Can’t believe it, Jean, who used to do those little “guess the squishy” giveaways, is now officially in the big two-zero era. Even though physically I’m not there next to you, I hope these words land as if you’re hearing them straight from my mouth. I genuinely pray that overwhelming happiness pours into your life like heavy rain. I hope every challenge and every moment of confusion you face becomes something you conquer with ease. I hope every good thing this world has to offer somehow finds its way to your side. And I also want to say I’m truly sorry if lately I’ve seemed distant, like I don’t care. Deep down, you already know the reason (gotta keep it halal, ukhtie). Also, forgive the late birthday gift, it’s still in process, coming soon, no worries. Anyway… I’m praying for nothing but the best for your life, and for the lives of the people you love most.

Every second spent with you feels like writing a piece of history that time could never erase. Today’s your special day, and I hope your smile shines a little brighter than it ever has before.
